# VPSA YOGA

> **Official Wholesale Banana Trading & Cold-Chain Logistics Web Platform**

VPSA YOGA (also known as *VPSA YOGA Banana Traders*) is a premier wholesale agricultural enterprise and temperature-controlled logistics hub based in Tamil Nadu, India. Direct sourcing from trusted farmers all over south india, the company provides farm-fresh, scientifically graded bananas with dedicated 13–14°C reefer transport across South India and international export markets.

---

## 🍌 Key Highlights

- **Direct Farm Sourcing:** Direct sourcing from trusted farmers all over south india with transparent wholesale pricing.
- **Dedicated Cold-Chain Logistics:** 13–14°C temperature-controlled transit ensuring harvest freshness and zero transit damage.
- **8 Premium Banana Varieties:** Red Banana (*Sevvazhai*), Poovan, Nendran, Yelakki, Karpuravalli, Rasthali, Robusta, and Monthan.
- **Multilingual Support:** Powered by Google Translate with instant translation across 28+ languages including Tamil, Hindi, Malayalam, Telugu, Kannada, Arabic, and global languages.
- **Export-Grade Standards:** International grading, hygienic packaging, and digital quotation processing.
