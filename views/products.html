Our Premium Banana Varieties

Hand-picked, scientifically graded, and cold-chain transported banana varieties sourced directly from trusted farmers all over south india.

Nutrient Superfruit

Red Banana (செவ்வாழை)

Rich in Vitamin C, B6 & Beta-Carotene

Rich in potassium, magnesium, vitamin B6, and higher levels of vitamin C and beta-carotene than yellow bananas. Known for immunity boosting, blood pressure regulation, and ocular health.

Taste Profile:Velvety berry sweetness
Shelf Life:6 to 8 Days
Ideal Temp:13.0°C - 14.5°C
Standard Packing:7kg / 13kg Corrugated Box
High Dietary Fiber

Poovan Banana (பூவன்)

Gentle on Stomach & Easy Digestion

Rich in dietary fiber, making it gentle on the stomach and exceptionally easy to digest. Enhances intestinal health, relieves acidity, and provides quick, sustained energy.

Taste Profile:Tangy-sweet, fragrant
Shelf Life:7 to 10 Days
Ideal Temp:13.5°C - 14.0°C
Standard Packing:10kg / 15kg Telescopic Cartons
Specialty

Nendran Banana (நேந்திரன்)

High Resistant Starch & Heart Health

High in resistant starch, dietary fiber, potassium, and Vitamin B6, making them beneficial for gut and heart health. Premier choice for banana chips and culinary delicacy.

Taste Profile:Rich, firm, versatile
Shelf Life:8 to 12 Days
Ideal Temp:13.0°C - 14.0°C
Standard Packing:12kg / 18kg Heavy Duty Crates
Honey Sweet Aroma

Yelakki Banana (ஏலக்கி)

Small, Highly Sweet & Aromatic Variety

Yelakki bananas are a small, highly sweet, and aromatic variety of banana widely cultivated in South India. Packed with energy, calcium, vitamin C, and fiber.

Taste Profile:Concentrated honey sweetness
Shelf Life:5 to 7 Days
Ideal Temp:13.5°C - 14.5°C
Standard Packing:5kg / 10kg Premium Cartons
Creamy & Traditional

Karpuravalli Banana (கற்பூரவள்ளி)

Plump, Golden-Yellow & Ash-Grey Flesh

Karpuravalli is a popular, traditional South Indian banana variety known for its plump, slightly angular shape, ash-grey or plump golden-yellow skin, and exceptionally sweet, creamy flesh.

Taste Profile:Creamy, fragrant sweetness
Shelf Life:6 to 9 Days
Ideal Temp:13.0°C - 14.0°C
Standard Packing:10kg Ventilated Export Boxes
Apple-Aroma Delicacy

Rasthali Banana (ரஸ்தாளி)

Silky Texture & Subtle Apple-Like Aroma

Rasthali is a premium, traditional Indian banana variety known for its exceptionally sweet taste, silky texture, and subtle apple-like aroma. High in digestive enzymes and vitamins.

Taste Profile:Silky sweet with apple aroma
Shelf Life:5 to 8 Days
Ideal Temp:13.5°C - 14.0°C
Standard Packing:8kg / 12kg Master Boxes
Export Cavendish

Robusta Cavendish (ரோபஸ்டா)

Global Favorite for Commercial Supply

Robusta is a widely consumed, medium-to-large Cavendish banana variety known for its sweet flavor, soft and creamy texture, and thick green peel that turns bright yellow upon ripening.

Taste Profile:Smooth, rich, classic
Shelf Life:14 to 21 Days @ 13.5°C
Ideal Temp:13.2°C - 14.0°C
Standard Packing:13.5kg / 18.14kg Reefer Boxes
Robust Cooking Variety

Monthan Banana (மொந்தன்)

Large Stocky Fruits & Starchy Mealy Pulp

Monthan banana is a popular, robust cooking banana variety in India known for its large, stocky, knobbed green fruits and starchy, mealy pulp. Ideal for savory culinary processing.

Taste Profile:Savory, starchy, firm
Shelf Life:15 to 25 Days
Ideal Temp:14.0°C - 16.0°C
Standard Packing:20kg / 25kg Ventilated Bags
