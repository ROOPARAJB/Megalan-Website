Privacy Policy & Data Notice

How VPSA YOGA FRISH collects, processes, and protects your business and personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

1. Introduction & Data Fiduciary Overview

Last Updated: October 2026 | Governing Law: Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

VPSA YOGA FRISH PVT LTD ("we", "our", or "the Company") acts as the Data Fiduciary regarding the personal data collected through this website (vpsayogafresh.com), quotation forms, WhatsApp channels, and direct wholesale inquiries. We are committed to processing your personal data lawfully, transparently, and solely for specified business purposes.

2. Personal Data We Collect

When you submit a Request for Quotation (RFQ) or contact our logistics desk, we collect only necessary and relevant information:

  • Full Name: To identify and address our business customer.
  • Business Email Address & Mobile Number: For quotation dispatch, logistics updates, and order confirmations.
  • Company / Trade Name & Delivery Destination: To calculate reefer transport route logistics and wholesale pricing.
  • Order Requirements & Quantities: To match inventory from our Oddanchatram & Theni farmer clusters.

3. Purpose Specification & Lawful Grounds for Processing

We process personal data solely on the lawful basis of your explicit consent and legitimate business fulfillment:

  • Generating custom commercial wholesale quotations and volume discount rates.
  • Scheduling temperature-controlled (13-14°C) cold-chain transit and dispatch notifications.
  • Maintaining commercial accounting records and mandatory GST compliance.
  • No Data Sale: We strictly do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, you have explicit statutory rights concerning your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about your business.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request permanent deletion of your inquiry record once commercial transactions conclude.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent for future trade communications at any time.

5. Security Safeguards & Data Retention

We enforce technical and operational safeguards including TLS encryption, server-side parameter sanitization, rate-limiting, and restricted access controls. Inactive inquiry records are safely purged after 12 months unless required for legal accounting records.
