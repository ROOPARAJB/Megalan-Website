About VPSA YOGA FRISH

Bridging two decades of agrarian trading heritage with state-of-the-art cold-chain logistics and cyber-secure supply management.

Who We Are

A South India Wholesale Powerhouse Built on Trust

We are VPSA YOGA Banana Traders, a Tamil Nadu-based wholesale fruit trading and logistics company. Started as a family business, we have grown into a reliable supply partner for hundreds of clients across South India.

With deep roots across agrarian belts in Theni, Oddanchatram, Trichy, and Kerala, we manage daily volume trading with end-to-end quality supervision.

What We Do

Our Core Capabilities & Services

🌾

Wholesale Banana Supply

Year-round bulk supply of 8 premium varieties: RED banana, Robusta, Nendran, Yelakki, Poovan, Karpuravalli, Rasthali, and Monthan.

🚜

Direct Farm Sourcing

Direct contracts with certified farm clusters in Theni, Oddanchatram, and Kerala. Eliminating middle-tier agents ensures superior farm realization and unbeatable wholesale prices.

Cold Chain & Reefer Fleet

Dedicated temperature-controlled fleet (13-14°C) with continuous telemetry, ethylene absorption, route optimization, and warehousing facilities.
